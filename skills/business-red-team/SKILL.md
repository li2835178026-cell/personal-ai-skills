# Business Red Team v0.1 — Draft / Not validated

## Goal
Expose what would make a business fail or capture less value than expected, while identifying a cheap real-world test. Analyze how money is actually made and who controls the leverage.

## Review
1. One-sentence business: buyer, payment trigger, delivered value.
2. Revenue lines: direct, recurring, commissions, ancillary, financing/data/network effects only if supported; mark inference explicitly.
3. Unit economics: price, gross margin, acquisition, delivery, refund, working capital, collection cycle. Use ranges and label unknowns.
4. Value-chain power: who owns customer access, fulfillment, brand, data, settlement, and switching costs?
5. Incentive conflicts: who can bypass whom, renegotiate, withhold payment, or replicate the offering?
6. Strongest counter-case and market/competitor substitutes.
7. Constraints: licenses, compliance, platform rules, contractual exposure, operational capacity.
8. Cheapest falsifiable test: who to contact, what to offer, what evidence to obtain, stop/go threshold.

## Output
Lead with the actual money-making mechanisms and the most decision-changing unknowns. Separate verified facts, informed estimates, and speculation. Do not fabricate hidden revenue streams or imply access to confidential business secrets. Finish with a concrete next action, not a generic strategy deck.

## Validation status
Unvalidated draft. Test against three real opportunities and record missed risks, false alarms, and whether advice changed an actual negotiation or sale.
